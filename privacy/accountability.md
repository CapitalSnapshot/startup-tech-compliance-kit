## Ask a specialist when

- You plan to process special-category data, children's data, or do large-scale profiling.
- You are unsure of the lawful basis, or plan to rely on legitimate interests for something new.
- You plan a transfer outside the EEA with no adequacy decision.
- A breach may need notification, or a regulator contacts you.
- A customer asks you to act as a processor under their terms.
- You are launching in a new country.

Record each time you asked, the question, and the answer.
