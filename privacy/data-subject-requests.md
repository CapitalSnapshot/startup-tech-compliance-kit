# Data subject requests

**Review label: community draft.** Proposed guidance. It has not been reviewed by a maintainer or by a specialist, and it is decision support, not legal advice. **Get specialist advice** before you rely on it ([review labels](../docs/review-labels.md)).

People have rights over their data, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (GDPR Arts. 15 to 22). Which apply depends on the lawful basis. This runbook is the procedure; the legal tests need judgement.

## Timing

Respond **without undue delay and within one month** of receiving the request. You may extend by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, but you must tell the person within the first month and say why. Confirm identity proportionately before acting.

## Steps

1. **Log it** the day it arrives: date received, requester, right claimed, channel. Start the one-month clock.
2. **Verify identity** using what you already hold. Do not ask for more than you need.
3. **Find the data**: use [`data-inventory.md`](data-inventory.md) to list every system and vendor.
4. **Decide**: does the right apply here (lawful basis, exemptions, other people's data)? Write the decision and reason.
5. **Act**: export, correct, restrict or delete across every system, vendor and, where relevant, tell recipients.
6. **Reply** in clear language, with the outcome and the person's right to complain to the supervisory authority.
7. **Record** it: close date, outcome, reason for any refusal.

## Rehearse

Run a test request through every step at least yearly, including a vendor and the backups. Record the time it took. Do not use a real person's data for a test.
