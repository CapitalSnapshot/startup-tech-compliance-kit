## How to fill it in

1. Open each control and its "Done when" list. Tick what is true today and **can be shown**.
2. Anything not ticked is a gap. Write it in one sentence.
3. Put the gap in the [risk register](../evidence/risk-register.md): score it, choose a treatment, assign an owner. Write the ID here.
4. Gaps you cannot assess yet are risks too: record "unknown" with a date to find out.
