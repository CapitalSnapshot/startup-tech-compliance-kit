# Maturity model

Four levels, so a founder can see progress without being told that finishing a checklist makes them compliant.

| Level | Name | What is true |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Baseline** | The critical safeguards are in place: MFA, secrets out of code, protected branches, backups, audit logs, a named incident owner |
| 2 | **Repeatable** | Processes have owners, schedules and dated evidence. The founder no longer has to remember them |
| 3 | **Measured** | Controls are tested on a schedule, exceptions are tracked, deficiencies are remediated, and the evidence check ([`../audit/`](../audit/)) runs regularly |
| 4 | **Assured** | The programme has had an independent assessment or audit, where required or commercially justified |

## How to read it

- **Level 4 cannot be reached by completing the kit.** It needs an independent assessment or audit by someone else. A checklist, an evidence check or a generated report never places you at Level 4.

- Levels describe a **control or area**, not the whole company. It is normal to be at level 3 on backups and level 1 on vendors.
- Level 4 is optional. Many companies never need it. Do not pursue it because the kit mentions it.
- Moving up a level means adding an owner, a schedule, evidence, a test, or an independent check. Not adding paperwork.
- Record where you are in your risk register or your management review notes, with a date.

## Quick self-placement

| Question | If yes |
|---|---|
| Is the safeguard actually switched on and tested once? | Level 1 |
| Does it have an owner, a schedule and a dated evidence file? | Level 2 |
| Is it tested on schedule, with exceptions tracked and fixed, and checked by the audit? | Level 3 |
| Has someone independent assessed it? | Level 4 |
