# Paths to a formal framework

> **Standing notice.** This is repository based, community maintained guidance. It is not legal advice, a formal compliance assessment, a certification or an independent security audit. Mappings and statements about frameworks are unverified unless their review label says otherwise ([review labels](../../docs/review-labels.md)).

Read [`../layers-and-outcomes.md`](../layers-and-outcomes.md) and [`../roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md) first. A framework is a **destination you choose** because a customer, contract, regulator or plan requires it, not because it is on a list.

| Path | Read when |
|---|---|
| [`soc2.md`](soc2.md) | A customer or investor asks for a SOC 2 report |
| [`iso27001.md`](iso27001.md) | A customer or partner asks for ISO/IEC 27001 certification, or you choose it |

Both pages describe a general process. They are **not** assessor-grade guidance: scope, timing and cost depend on your business and your assessor, so use them to prepare the conversation, not to replace it.

For GDPR there is no certificate to earn. Follow [`../../privacy/`](../../privacy/) and take legal advice.
