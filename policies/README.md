# Policies

Short policy templates for the governance layer. Keep each to a page, **write what you actually do**, then do it. A policy nobody follows is worse than none, and a long one nobody reads is the same.

For an early-stage team most of these start as a page or less, backed by real practice. Date them, assign an owner, have people acknowledge them, and review yearly. They are evidence for [`../controls/12-risk-and-governance.md`](../controls/12-risk-and-governance.md).

| Policy | What it sets |
|---|---|
| [Information security policy](information-security-policy.md) | Sets the direction: why you protect information, who is accountable, and the rules everyone follows. |
| [Acceptable use policy](acceptable-use-policy.md) | What people may and may not do with company systems, data and devices. |
| [Onboarding and offboarding](onboarding-and-offboarding.md) | The same steps every time someone joins, changes role or leaves. |
| [Security awareness training](security-awareness-training.md) | What everyone learns, when, and how it is recorded. |
| [Data classification and handling](data-classification-and-handling.md) | How you label data and what each label requires. |
| [Business continuity and disaster recovery](business-continuity-and-disaster-recovery.md) | How you keep running, and how you come back, after a serious disruption. |
| [Secure development policy](secure-development-policy.md) | How code is written, reviewed, built and released. |
| [Encryption and key management](encryption-and-key-management.md) | What is encrypted, how, and how keys are looked after. |
| [Roles and responsibilities](roles-and-responsibilities.md) | Who is accountable for what. One person may hold several roles; write that down. |

The [vulnerability remediation standard](vulnerability-remediation-standard.md) sets discovery, severity, deadlines, owners, exceptions, verification and escalation. Vulnerability management is a control, not only a policy: see [`../controls/11-vulnerability-management.md`](../controls/11-vulnerability-management.md).
