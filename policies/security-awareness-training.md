## Policy

1. At joining and yearly: phishing and social engineering, password and MFA practice, handling customer and personal data, reporting incidents, safe use of AI tools.
2. Role-specific: engineers on secure development and secrets; support on identity checks and data subject requests.
3. Record: name (or role), date, topic, how completion was checked.
4. After an incident: a short targeted refresher.
