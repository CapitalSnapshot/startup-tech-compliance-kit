## Policy

1. Use company accounts only; never share logins; MFA stays on.
2. Devices: disk encryption on, screen lock, updates applied, no unmanaged devices for production access.
3. Never put secrets, customer data or personal data into chat, tickets, public repos or unapproved AI tools.
4. Approved software and services only; ask before adding a vendor ([`../controls/07-vendor-management.md`](../controls/07-vendor-management.md)).
5. Report loss of a device or suspected compromise at once.
6. The company may monitor company systems as described in <where>.
