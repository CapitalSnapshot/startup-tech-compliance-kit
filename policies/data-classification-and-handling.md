## Policy

1. **Public**: may be published. **Internal**: for staff. **Confidential**: customer data, contracts, source code. **Restricted**: personal data of special categories, credentials, keys.
2. Handling by class: where it may be stored, who may access it, whether it may be emailed or copied, encryption required, retention.
3. Production data stays in production ([`../controls/04-environment-separation.md`](../controls/04-environment-separation.md)).
4. Label by default as Confidential when unsure.
5. Disposal: how each class is deleted ([`../privacy/retention-and-deletion.md`](../privacy/retention-and-deletion.md)).
