## Policy

1. Purpose and scope: what this covers (people, systems, data).
2. Accountability: <role> is accountable for information security; <role> approves exceptions.
3. Principles: least privilege, separation of environments, tested backups, secrets only in the secrets manager, changes only through the pipeline.
4. Compliance with laws and contracts: we identify those that apply ([`../scoping/`](../scoping/)).
5. Incidents: report suspected incidents to <contact> immediately; no blame for good-faith reports.
6. Exceptions: request in writing, approved by <role>, time-limited, recorded in the risk register.
7. Review: this policy is reviewed yearly and after a significant change.
