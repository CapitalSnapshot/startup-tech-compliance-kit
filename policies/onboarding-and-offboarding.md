The same steps every time someone joins, changes role or leaves.

Keep it to one page. Write what you actually do, then do it.

## Policy

1. **Joining:** agreement signed (including confidentiality); named accounts created with least privilege; MFA enrolled; device issued and encrypted; policies read and acknowledged; awareness session done; access recorded.
2. **Changing role:** review access and remove what is no longer needed the same week.
3. **Leaving:** remove all access on the **last day**; rotate any shared secret they knew; recover the device; revoke tokens and keys; confirm in the access review; record the date.
4. Keep a dated record for each person ([`../evidence/access-review.md`](../evidence/access-review.md)).

## Acknowledgement

People with access confirm in writing (name or role, date) that they have read it.
