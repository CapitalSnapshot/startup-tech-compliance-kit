# Risk management

A risk register is the working record of what could go wrong, how bad it would be, what you decided to do about it, and who answers for the decision. Read [`methodology.md`](methodology.md), then keep [`../evidence/risk-register.md`](../evidence/risk-register.md).

Why it matters: it is the one place a reviewer, customer or auditor can see that you **prioritise** risk rather than react to it, and that the decisions were taken by someone with the authority to take them.
