# Before the first customer

- [ ] Controls 1 to 5 in [`../controls/`](../controls/) are done, with evidence.
- [ ] Dev and production are separate, and no real data exists outside production.
- [ ] Production deploys only from the pipeline, with signed artefacts verified on the host.
- [ ] A restore test has passed, with timings.
- [ ] Alerts exist and have recipients ([`../controls/06-logging-and-monitoring.md`](../controls/06-logging-and-monitoring.md)).
- [ ] A privacy notice states what you really do. A record of processing exists.
- [ ] A data-processing agreement is signed with every vendor that handles personal data.
- [ ] You can answer a deletion request, and you have rehearsed it.
- [ ] The incident plan is one page and known to everyone with production access.
